Dhofar University Academic Regulation Summary: Under DU undergraduate academic regulations, any student whose cumulative GPA falls below 65.0% is placed on Academic Probation. Students on First Probation must achieve a semester GPA ≥ 65.0% to avoid progression to Second Probation. Failure to raise cumulative GPA after repeated probations leads to Strict Probation and potential dismissal. Students on probation may register for a maximum of 12 credit hours unless granted special approval by the Dean.
