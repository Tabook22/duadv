Articulation & Course Transfer Bylaws

Under official Dhofar University academic regulations, high-performing Diploma graduates have direct pathways to transition into any Department of Computer Science Bachelor of Science degree.

B.Sc. Cybersecurity

Direct transfer of up to 60 credit hours; students advance directly to Year 3 core subjects.

B.Sc. Data Science

Full articulation for programming and math courses; enter directly into Machine Learning sequence.

B.Sc. Computer Science

Standard software & systems pathway; complete upper-division requirements in ~4 semesters.
