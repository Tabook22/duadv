Institutional Recommendations for the Department Chairperson

To systematically reverse the academic decline of these {{ report.total_at_risk }} students and facilitate their elevation back into Good Standing (CGPA ≥ 65.0%), I respectfully recommend the implementation of the following administrative measures:

  1. Strict Registration Load Controls (DU Article 22): Direct the registration registrar and department advisors to reject all course overload requests exceeding 12 credit hours for any student on First, Second, or Strict Probation.
  2. Priority Seat Allocation for Gatekeeper Prerequisite Repeats: Ensure that sections for repeated core courses (particularly MATH 199, CMPS 110N, and CMPS 180) maintain reserved capacity for probation students, enabling them to capitalize on the DU Article 18 grade replacement multiplier.
  3. Structured Department Tutorial Lab Hours: Require lab instructors and teaching assistants to conduct mandatory weekly tutorial review sessions in programming and calculus, submitting bi-weekly attendance rosters to the academic advisors.
  4. Midterm Academic Audit & Article 24 Safeguard: Mandate a departmental progress check following Week 6 assessments. For any student on Strict or Second Probation scoring under 60% by Week 9, coordinate with the advisor to initiate an official Course Withdrawal (W) before the Week 10 deadline to avert grade failure and dismissal.
