Dear Chairperson,

I am formally submitting this academic diagnostic briefing and intervention dossier concerning the advisees under my supervision who are currently placed on Academic Probation (First, Second, and Strict/Third Probation) or categorized as academically at risk at Dhofar University.

The objective of this briefing is to present the Department Chairperson with full visibility into the student cohort's academic status, delineate their study plan progress (Diploma in Computer Science vs. Bachelor of Science in Computer Science), and articulate evidence-based rescue action plans to elevate their Cumulative GPA above the mandatory 65.0% Good Standing benchmark in accordance with Dhofar University Academic Regulations Article 14, Article 18, and Article 22.

  5. Strict Registration Load Controls (DU Article 22): Direct the registration registrar and department advisors to reject all course overload requests exceeding 12 credit hours for any student on First, Second, or Strict Probation.
