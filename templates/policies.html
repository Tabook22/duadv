Quick Advisor Reference: Dhofar University Core Academic Rules
Rule 1 • Academic Probation
Cumulative GPA < 65.0%

Any student with CGPA below 65.0% is placed on Academic Probation. Strictly restricted to max 12 credit hours in following semester.

Rule 2 • Repeating Courses
Grade Replacement (F/D)

Students may repeat courses with F, D, or D+. The higher grade replaces the earlier grade in cumulative GPA calculation. Prioritize repeating prerequisite courses.

Rule 3 • Course Load Limits
15–18 CH (Normal)

Normal semester load is 15-18 credits. Probation: 12 credits max. Graduating seniors can take up to 21 credits with Dean authorization.

Rule 4 • Probation Removal
CGPA ≥ 65.0% Clear

Once cumulative GPA reaches 65.0%, student is returned to Good Standing with standard course load allowances restored.
